# Google Play Custom Store Listings — en-US

English localization makes Jingdu usable and discoverable on non-Chinese system locales without changing the product scope: Jingdu remains a privacy-first offline TXT reader with deep Chinese-text support.

## Default
- **Name:** Jingdu - Offline TXT Reader
- **Intent:** offline/local TXT reader with Chinese encoding and cleanup depth
- **Hero:** Open messy TXT correctly. Clean distractions locally. Read long files comfortably.

## `txt-reader`
- Search intent: TXT reader, offline TXT reader, local text reader
- Screenshots: Open local TXT → large-file reading → chapters/search/bookmarks

## `txt-encoding`
- Search intent: garbled TXT, GBK reader, GB18030, Big5
- Screenshots: Fix garbled TXT → UTF-8/GBK/GB18030/Big5/UTF-16 → re-decode without finding the source file again

## `smart-clean`
- Search intent: clean TXT, remove novel ads/watermarks, text cleanup
- Screenshots: Find repeated promos/watermarks → preview count/confidence → apply selected suggestions → reuse global rules

## `local-novel`
- Search intent: offline novel reader, local novel reader, private reader
- Screenshots: Books stay on your device → no account/no upload → offline read-aloud/auto page/sleep timer

Do not introduce claims for EPUB, cloud sync, online catalogs, subscriptions or uploaded AI processing.
